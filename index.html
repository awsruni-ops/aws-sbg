Who it's for

Is this for me?

  • You are curious about the cloud and want to see how it really works.
  • You are a beginner. No experience needed.
  • You learn best by building something yourself.
  • You want to meet other students and people from the industry.
FAQ

What everyone asks

Do I need experience?

No. The weekly track is for people starting from zero. Curiosity is all you need.

How do I join?

Registration for the current round is closed. Every update is posted on Instagram, so follow us to hear first.

Will there be mentors?

Yes. Experienced industry professionals will join to share their experience, give tips, and answer questions about working in the field.

When does it start?

The weekly club meets every Wednesday at 18:00. Start dates and any changes are announced on the group's Instagram.
